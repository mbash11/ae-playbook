Four tools. Free tiers. Installed in 30 minutes. Here is exactly what SaaS AEs are using to get 10+ hours of selling time back every week.

The average SaaS account executive spends less than 35% of their working week actually selling. The rest goes on CRM updates, follow-up emails, pre-call research, pipeline reports, and internal handoffs. None of it moves deals forward.

AI tools for account executives have changed that equation significantly in the past 12 months. This guide covers the four AI tools that are actually worth your time as a SaaS AE, what each one does, and exactly how to use them in your daily workflow.

What makes an AI tool actually useful for account executives?

Most AI tool guides recommend software that sounds impressive but creates more friction than it removes. For an account executive, a useful AI tool meets three criteria:

  • It slots into your existing workflow. You should not have to rebuild how you work to use it. The best AI tools for AEs fit around what you are already doing.
  • It handles repeatable tasks. AI excels at consistent inputs producing consistent outputs. Post-call follow-ups, pre-call research briefs, and pipeline summaries all qualify. Strategic deal decisions do not.
  • It has a free tier that is genuinely useful. You should not need IT sign-off or a company credit card to get started.

The four AI tools every SaaS AE should be using

Fathom
Call recording + CRM logging

Fathom records, transcribes, and summarises every sales call automatically. The summary includes key discussion points, objections raised, and agreed next steps — and it pushes directly to your CRM without you touching it. The free tier has no time limits and no call caps.

Setup time: 8 minutes · fathom.video

Fathom is the single highest impact AI tool for account executives because it eliminates the two most time-consuming post-call tasks simultaneously. Most AEs spend 20 to 25 minutes after every call updating their CRM and writing a follow-up email. With Fathom the CRM is updated automatically before you have moved on to your next call.

Perplexity
Pre-call research

Perplexity is a real-time web search tool that synthesises results into a single clean output. For pre-call research it replaces 20 to 30 minutes of tab-hopping across LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Google News, and company websites with two targeted searches that take under five minutes.

Setup time: 3 minutes · perplexity.ai

The key difference between Perplexity and a standard Google search is synthesis. Rather than returning ten links you have to read individually, Perplexity reads them for you and gives you the relevant information in one place. For pre-call research on a prospect account that is the difference between 30 minutes of tab-hopping and a five-minute brief.

Claude or ChatGPT
Drafting + structuring

A large language model is the engine behind your follow-up emails, CRM notes, pipeline summaries, and pre-call briefs. Both Claude and ChatGPT have free tiers that are more than sufficient for the prompts in a standard AE workflow. The key is having well-structured prompts rather than typing from scratch each time.

Setup time: 5 minutes · claude.ai or chat.openai.com

The quality of your output from any AI drafting tool is determined almost entirely by the quality of your prompt. Generic input produces generic output. The prompts that work for SaaS AEs are specific, structured, and built around the patterns of real sales conversations — not generic business writing tasks.

Your CRM's native AI
Already paid for

Salesforce, HubSpot, and most major CRM platforms now have built-in AI features for deal scoring, activity summaries, and forecast commentary. Most AEs have never opened the AI features in their CRM because they were not there two years ago. Spend 20 minutes exploring what is already available in your platform before adding anything new.

Setup time: 20 minutes · already in your CRM

How to use these tools together as a SaaS AE

The four tools above are most powerful when they work as a system rather than as individual point solutions. Here is how they fit together in a typical selling day:

  • Before a call: Two Perplexity searches on the company and the contact's role. One Claude prompt turns that research into a structured six-point brief covering company context, likely pain points, probable objections, and a sharp opening question.
  • During the call: Fathom records and transcribes automatically in the background. Nothing to manage.
  • After the call: Fathom summary appears within minutes. One Claude prompt turns it into a personalised follow-up email. Another structures the CRM notes. Total post-call time: five minutes instead of twenty-five.
  • End of week: Three Claude prompts compress two hours of pipeline admin into a 20-minute Friday ritual covering health checks, deal commentary, and the manager report.

What AI tools for account executives cannot do

It is worth being clear about where AI tools reach their limits in a sales context. AI does not replace the judgment that closes deals. It does not build the trust that keeps relationships warm. It does not read a room, handle a procurement objection, or decide whether to push for a close or give a prospect more time.

What it does do is handle the administrative layer that sits between those high-value activities and your calendar — and do it faster and more consistently than any human can manage while also running a full pipeline.
