How to Use AI for Post-Call Follow-Up as a SaaS AE

Five copy-paste prompts that handle your follow-up email and CRM notes in under five minutes. No more losing 25 minutes after every call to admin.

Post-call admin is one of the biggest silent killers of selling time for SaaS account executives. After a one-hour call, most AEs spend an additional 20 to 25 minutes updating their CRM, writing a follow-up email, and logging next steps. At four calls per day that is up to two hours of post-call admin before you have done anything proactive.

AI changes this equation completely. With the right setup, post-call follow-up as a SaaS AE takes five minutes rather than twenty-five. Here is exactly how to do it.

25
Minutes lost per call on post-call admin.At four calls per day, that is 100 minutes of admin time before you have sent a single proactive outreach. AI reduces this to five minutes per call — returning roughly six hours of selling time per week from this task alone.

Step 1 — Set up Fathom for automatic CRM logging

Before anything else, install Fathom at fathom.video. It is free with no call limits. Connect it to your calendar and your CRM during setup — the process takes eight minutes.

From that point forward, every call you take through your calendar will be automatically recorded, transcribed, and summarised. Fathom pushes the summary directly to your CRM as a logged activity, updates the contact record, and captures the key discussion points and next steps from the conversation.

You do not need to do anything during or after the call for this to happen. The CRM is updated before you have opened your laptop to start typing.

Step 2 — Use the Fathom summary as your AI input

After your call ends, Fathom sends you a summary email and makes the transcript available in its dashboard. This summary is the input for every AI follow-up prompt below. Copy it and paste it directly into Claude or ChatGPT.

Do not paste raw transcripts. The summary is cleaner, more structured, and produces significantly better output from your AI tool than an unedited transcript.

The five post-call follow-up prompts

Save all five of these in a document you can access from any device. Over time you will know which one to reach for based on the type of call you just had.

Prompt 1 — After a discovery call
"You are an experienced SaaS AE. I just finished a discovery call. Here is the summary: [paste Fathom summary]. My product helps [company type] with [value prop]. Write a follow-up email under 200 words. Conversational tone. Thanks them briefly, summarises key discussion points, confirms next steps, ends with a clear CTA."
Prompt 2 — After a demo
"SaaS AE here. Just delivered a demo. Summary: [paste Fathom summary]. Prospect most interested in [feature]. Main concern: [objection]. Write a follow-up under 200 words that acknowledges their interest, addresses the concern briefly, and proposes a next step."
Prompt 3 — After an objection-heavy call
"SaaS AE. Tough call with several objections. Summary: [paste Fathom summary]. Biggest objection: [objection]. Write a follow-up under 180 words. Acknowledge honestly, offer one supporting point, propose a specific next step. Not defensive. Not overselling."
Prompt 4 — After a commercial conversation
"SaaS AE. Commercial conversation, prospect close to decision. Summary: [paste Fathom summary]. We discussed pricing and [what was agreed]. Write a follow-up under 200 words confirming what was discussed, next steps with owners, tone of confidence not pressure."
Prompt 5 — When nothing was decided
"SaaS AE. Call with no clear next step agreed. Summary: [paste Fathom summary]. Write a follow-up under 150 words. Honest about where we are, proposes a specific next step with timeframe, keeps door open without being desperate."

The CRM notes prompt

In addition to the follow-up email, use this prompt to generate structured CRM notes you can paste directly into your opportunity record:

CRM notes prompt
"Here is a summary of a sales call I just had: [paste Fathom summary]. Write structured CRM notes covering: current deal stage, key discussion points (3 bullets max), main objection raised, agreed next steps with owner and timeframe, deal health rating 1-10 with one sentence of reasoning."
